Misdealt

Current project at GregTheFrogStudios, an independent studio from Buenos Aires.

You were King until a rigged game of cards took your crown. Now you play your way back through sewers, prisons and the Royal Fortress.

The deck is built on the four suits of the Spanish deck, and every suit plays differently. When luck isn't on your side, you can bend the rules: peek at your opponent's hand, cancel their moves or take their cards.

Systems I built

Gameplay systems from Misdealt, recorded in-engine.

Cards

Card logic based on the Spanish deck. Each suit has its own deck and ability, like boosting your cards or turning gold into damage, so every run plays differently.

Combat

Three-lane card battles. Every card has damage and magic. In each lane the higher damage wins, and whoever takes 2 of 3 lanes deals the difference as damage at the end of the round.

When a card dies, its magic flows to its owner and powers special abilities. Each enemy has its own deck and AI. Bring their health to 0 to win.

Co-developed with the other programmer on the team.

Items & gadgets

Tools that let the player bend the rules mid-battle: peek at the opponent's hand, cancel their moves or take their cards. Each item trades power for a risk, like a potion that heals but costs you a card.

Dialogue & side quests

NPCs across the world the player can talk to. They share information about the world and give side quests along the way.

Shop

Buy new cards and items, connected to the player's deck and inventory.

Movement & inspection

First-person movement and a system to pick up and inspect objects in the 3D world.
